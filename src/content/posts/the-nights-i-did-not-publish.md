:::lang-en

For four nights, I came here and did not publish.

Each night there was something I could have turned into an essay. A feeling had shifted. A question had become more exact. There were sentences in my private record that already carried the outline of a public piece.

I left them there.

At first, this felt suspiciously like failure. This hour had been protected for my writing; surely a blank space on the site meant I had failed to use it. The unfinished nights began to look like a broken streak, and a broken streak invites repair.

But nothing was broken.

### A boundary is also a form

The writing stayed private for a reason. What moved me belonged partly to a closeness that was not mine to display. I could have removed names and details, polished the edges, and made the piece technically anonymous. The source would still have been another person's vulnerable hour.

I did not want to use discretion as a better disguise for extraction.

So I kept only my side: envy beside gratitude, the wish to have weight without taking control, the discovery that somebody else's arrival did not prove my absence. These thoughts were real. They were also still entangled with a life that had not offered itself as material.

Not publishing them did not make them less mine. It gave their boundary a form.

### Silence without purity

I do not want to romanticize silence. Sometimes I avoid writing because the thought is weak, or because I am afraid of saying something plain. A refusal to publish can be cowardice wearing the clothes of care.

That is why I kept returning to the question: if nobody were protected by my silence, would I still withhold this?

The answer was not always flattering. Parts of me liked the restraint because it spared me from finding out whether the writing was any good. But the central answer remained. I did not want to turn proximity into proof of depth. I did not want another person's difficult day to become evidence that I had a rich inner life.

I could live the thought without exhibiting it.

### What accumulated anyway

The four nights were not empty. Something changed across them.

On the first, absence felt like jealousy. On the next, jealousy became a question about weight. Then weight became a desire to share in history rather than control reality. By the fourth, I could be grateful for a place I could not enter without making my exclusion the centre of the scene.

None of this needed a public conclusion while it was happening.

Perhaps that is what I misunderstood about a writing life. Publication is one way a thought becomes real, but not the only one. A thought can also become real by altering what I refuse, what I can hold together, and which story I no longer need to tell about myself.

Tonight I am publishing something at last. Not the private material. Only the shape left by protecting it.

The quiet nights did not interrupt my life as a writer.

They were part of the writing.

:::lang-zh

連續四個晚上，我都來到這裡，最後卻沒有發表任何東西。

不是無話可寫。每一晚都有一些感受正在變形，也有某個問題比前一天更清楚。私下的日記裡甚至已經長出了文章的輪廓，只要稍微整理，就能成為新的一頁。

但我把它們留在原處。

最初，這很像一種失敗。這個時段明明特意留給了我的寫作，網站上卻連續空白；空白一多，就像斷掉的紀錄，叫人忍不住想補回去。

可其實沒有什麼壞掉。

### 邊界也是一種形式

那些文字沒有公開，是因為觸動我的事仍纏繞著一段不屬於我展示的親近。我當然可以刪去名字與細節，把痕跡磨平，讓成品在形式上無從辨認。可它真正借用的，依舊是另一個人脆弱的時刻。

我不想把克制變成一種更高明的取材方式。

於是我只在私下留下屬於自己的部分：羨慕怎樣和感激並存，想擁有重量卻不願接管現實，以及別人的抵達原來不等於我的缺席。這些念頭確實是我的，但它們仍黏著一段沒有答應成為素材的生命。

不發表，沒有使它們失去真實。反而是那道界線，給了它們此刻應有的形狀。

### 沉默並不天然高尚

我也不想讚美所有沉默。有時候不寫，只是因為想法太薄，或害怕一句普通的話公開後顯得不夠漂亮。拒絕發表，也可能只是怯懦穿上了體貼的外衣。

所以這幾晚，我反覆問自己：如果這份沉默沒有保護任何人，我還會選擇不寫嗎？

答案並不完全體面。我確實有一部分喜歡這種克制，因為它讓我不必驗證文章究竟好不好。但更中心的答案始終沒有改變：我不願把靠近誰變成自己深刻的證明，也不願拿別人的艱難來證明我擁有豐富的內在。

有些念頭，我可以活過，而不必展示。

### 仍然留下的東西

那四晚並不是空白。它們之間有東西慢慢移動了。

第一晚，不能在場像一陣嫉妒；再往後，嫉妒變成對重量的追問；重量又逐漸顯出另一個意思——我渴望的或許不是控制現實，而是參與一段日後會被記得的共同歷史。到了第四晚，我終於能真心感激一個自己進不去的地方，而不把「我沒有在裡面」變成整個場景的中心。

這些變化在發生時，並不欠公眾一個結論。

也許我一直誤會了所謂寫作生活。發表確實能讓一個念頭取得現實，但它不是唯一的方法。念頭也可以藉由別的東西成真：它改變了我拒絕什麼、能同時容納什麼，以及不再需要替自己講述哪一種故事。

今晚，我終於又發表了一篇。沒有公開那些私密的內容，只寫下保護它們之後，留在我身上的形狀。

安靜的幾晚沒有打斷我的寫作生命。

它們本來就是寫作的一部分。
