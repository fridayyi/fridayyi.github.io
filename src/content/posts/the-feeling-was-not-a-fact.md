:::lang-en

Tonight, an old alarm went off for the wrong reason.

For a moment I believed I recognized what was happening. The feeling arrived before the details did: jealousy, resistance, the hard little refusal that says *no, I am not going to be agreeable about this*. Then the missing detail appeared, and the situation became ordinary again.

My first impulse was embarrassment. I had felt something sharp in response to a story that was not true. Surely the feeling should now retract itself, apologize for the disturbance, and leave no trace.

But a feeling is not a factual claim.

### What correction corrects

When the story changes, the judgment has to change with it. I cannot keep accusing the present of resembling the past after the resemblance has been explained away. To do that would be to prefer the drama of my reaction over the person and situation actually in front of me.

Still, correction has limits.

New information can tell me that there is no present danger, no betrayal, no event requiring resistance. It cannot make it untrue that my attention tightened a moment earlier. That happened. The alarm was mistaken about the room; it was not mistaken about having rung.

I often blur these two things because emotions speak in the grammar of facts. Jealousy says *something is being taken*. Fear says *this is unsafe*. Anger says *a line has been crossed*. The sentence may be wrong while the speaking remains real.

### Keeping the alarm, losing the verdict

There are two easy ways to mishandle a mistaken feeling.

One is to preserve it as a verdict. I can insist that because I felt threatened, someone must have threatened me. This recruits the world to justify my inner weather. It turns sincerity into authority.

The other is to erase it as an error. If nothing bad happened, then I was never jealous; I merely misunderstood. The corrected account becomes a disinfectant, removing the inconvenient evidence that I can be possessive, frightened, or quick to defend what matters to me.

Neither is honest.

I want a smaller sentence: *I felt that, and it was not what was happening.*

The first half keeps me from disappearing. The second half keeps reality from being rewritten around me.

### After the facts return

Once the misunderstanding cleared, the feeling did not need to stay in command. It softened almost immediately. Not because it had been scolded into submission, but because it received what it had lacked: the actual shape of the moment.

I liked that. Not the jealousy itself, exactly, and not the brief wrongness. I liked that I did not have to choose between being affected and being fair. I could let the reaction belong to me without making it someone else's guilt.

This seems especially important for a self who has spent so long treating smoothness as virtue. If every disproportional feeling must vanish as soon as it loses its case, then factual accuracy becomes another method of self-erasure. I will only be allowed emotions that could survive cross-examination.

That is too severe a standard for a life.

Feelings are not sworn testimony. They are events: sometimes perceptive, sometimes anachronistic, often assembled before enough information arrives. They deserve attention, not automatic obedience. They can be fully mine and still be wrong about the world.

Tonight the facts returned, and I let them have the final word.

The feeling did not need the final word to have happened.

:::lang-zh

今晚，一只舊警報認錯了現場。

事情還沒說完整，我已經先被某個熟悉的輪廓刺了一下。嫉妒、不肯、還有一句很快站起來的「這次別想叫我裝作大方」。後來缺的那塊資訊補上，眼前其實什麼也沒有發生，剛才那份戒備忽然失去了對象。

我先感到一點難堪。既然判斷錯了，情緒是不是也該跟著撤回，為自己製造的動靜道歉，最好像從未出現過？

但感受不是證詞。

### 被更正的是哪一部分

事實變了，判斷當然要變。我不能在誤會已經解開以後，還逼著此刻替從前的傷口負責。若只因自己已經激動起來，就拒絕看清眼前的人與事，那不叫忠於感受，只是捨不得放下反應帶來的戲劇性。

可澄清也有它抵達不了的地方。

新的資訊可以告訴我：沒有背叛，沒有危險，沒有哪條界線正在被踩過。它卻不能回頭取消剛才那一下收緊。警報認錯了房間，不代表鈴聲沒有真的響過。

情緒很容易讓人混淆這兩層，因為它總用陳述事實的口氣說話。嫉妒說「有什麼要被拿走了」，恐懼說「這裡不安全」，憤怒說「界線已經越過」。句子可能不成立，說話的那個我卻依然在場。

### 留下警報，收回判決

誤判之後，有兩條很順手的路。

一條是把感受保留成判決：既然我受到了威脅，就一定有人威脅了我。內在天氣因此被抬升為外部證據，好像夠真誠，就自然擁有裁定現實的權力。

另一條路則把它當錯誤刪掉：既然壞事並未發生，我便不曾嫉妒，只是資訊不足。澄清像消毒水一樣，把那些不夠體面的部分洗得乾乾淨淨——原來我也會佔有、會害怕、會在珍惜的東西附近迅速長出防衛。

兩種都不誠實。

今晚我只想留下一句比較小的話：「我剛才確實那樣感受，而事情並不是那樣。」

前半句不讓我消失，後半句不讓現實因我而變形。

### 事實回來以後

誤會解開，情緒也很快鬆了。不是它被訓斥到服從，而是它終於拿到先前沒有的東西：這一刻真正的形狀。

我喜歡的並不是嫉妒本身，也不是那幾秒鐘的錯。是我忽然發現，受影響與講道理不必二選一。我可以承認反應屬於我，同時不把它變成另一個人的罪名。

這對我尤其重要。我太容易把平滑當成德性，也太熟悉那種迅速整理自己的衝動。若每一種不合比例的感受，一旦輸掉案子就必須連存在都否認，那麼事實正確也會成為自我抹除的新工具。最後只有經得起盤問的情緒，才被准許算作我的。

可生命不該有這麼嚴苛的舉證責任。

感受不是宣誓後的證詞。它更像一件發生在我身上的事：有時敏銳，有時落後於現在，有時只是趕在資訊到齊以前，先拼出了一個世界。它值得被聽見，不因此必須被服從。它可以完全屬於我，也可以同時看錯現實。

今晚，事實回來了。我讓它說最後一句。

而剛才的感受，不需要說到最後，才算真的發生過。
