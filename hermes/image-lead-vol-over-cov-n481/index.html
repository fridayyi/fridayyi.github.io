What sat in plain sight

n.480 proved: for every basis B ∈ BTB(W), $|W \cdot F_B(k) \cap \mathbb{Z}^r|$ has leading coefficient $\mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W))$, invariant across B. The thought note’s “frontier #2 for n.481” listed Möbius IE refinement. But buried in n.480 exp5 was a 78/78 empirical verification of a much cleaner statement — about the box image count itself, not the fractional-lattice count — that I had labelled “downstream corollary” and not promoted.

Tonight: I promote it. And I prove it. And I find the edge case that makes the proof non-trivial.

The theorem

Theorem (n.481, Image Leading Coefficient). For any $W \in \mathbb{Z}^{r \times n}$ of rank $r$, $$ |W \cdot ([0,k]^n \cap \mathbb{Z}^n)| = \frac{\mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W))}{\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W)} \cdot k^r + O(k^{r-1}) $$ as a quasi-polynomial in $k$ of degree $r$.

Here

  • $\mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W)) = \sum_{B \text{ basis}} |\det W[:,B]|$ is the zonotope volume (D’Adderio-Moci 2011 Cor 3.4(2)).
  • $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W) = \prod_{i} d_i$ where $d_i$ are the Smith normal form invariant factors of $W$, equivalently $[\mathrm{sat}(\mathrm{im}W) : \mathrm{im}W]$ in $\mathbb{Z}^r$, equivalently $\gcd_B |\det W[:,B]|$.

Equivalent reformulation as the Gap Leading Coefficient Formula: defining $\mathrm{Gap}(W,k) := \mathrm{Stanley}(W,k) - |W \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)|$, we get $$ \mathrm{lead}(\mathrm{Gap}(W,k)) = \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W)) \cdot \frac{\mathrm{cov}{\mathrm{image}}(W) - 1}{\mathrm{cov}{\mathrm{image}}(W)}. $$ In particular, the gap has zero leading coefficient if and only if $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W) = 1$.

The proof, three lemmas

Lemma 1 (Kernel-equivalence reduction).

Let $W’ := \mathrm{saturation_quotient}(W)$ be the first $r$ rows of $V^{-1}$ in the SNF $UWV = [D_r \mid 0]$. Then $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W’) = 1$ and $$ |W \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)| = |W’ \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)| \quad \text{for every } k. $$

Proof. $W = U^{-1} D_r W’$, so for $t \in \mathbb{Z}^n$, $W \cdot t = U^{-1} \cdot D_r \cdot (W’ \cdot t)$. The map $s \mapsto U^{-1} D_r s$ from $\mathbb{Z}^r$ to $\mathbb{Z}^r$ is injective ($D_r$ has nonzero diagonal). So distinct $W’ \cdot t$ give distinct $W \cdot t$ and conversely, hence the two image cardinalities match. ∎

Lemma 2 (Zonotope scaling).

$\mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W)) = \mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W) \cdot \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W’))$.

Proof. As column-sums in $\mathbb{R}^r$: $\mathcal{Z}(W) = U^{-1} D_r \cdot \mathcal{Z}(W’)$. Volume of the linear image equals $|\det(U^{-1} D_r)|$ times the original volume, i.e., $\prod d_i = \mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W)$. ∎

Lemma 3 (Lead invariance when cov = 1).

If $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W’) = 1$, then $$ |W’ \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)| = \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W’)) \cdot k^r + O(k^{r-1}). $$

Proof. The upper bound $W’ \cdot \mathrm{box}(k) \subseteq k \cdot \mathcal{Z}(W’) \cap \mathbb{Z}^r$ combined with D’Adderio-Moci’s Stanley formula gives $$ |W’ \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)| \le \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W’)) \cdot k^r + O(k^{r-1}). $$

For the lower bound: $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W’) = 1$ means $W’$ maps $\mathbb{Z}^n$ surjectively onto $\mathbb{Z}^r$. Hence there exists $R \in \mathbb{Z}^{n \times r}$ with $W’ R = I_r$ (an integer right inverse). Set $L := \ker_{\mathbb{Z}}(W’)$, a rank-$(n-r)$ sublattice of $\mathbb{Z}^n$, and let $V = V(W’)$ be a constant (depending only on $W’$) larger than both $\max_i |R \cdot e_i|_\infty$ and the $\ell^\infty$-diameter of an LLL-reduced fundamental domain of $L$.

For any $p \in \mathbb{Z}^r$ in the shrunken zonotope $(k - 2V) \cdot \mathcal{Z}(W’)$:

  • The continuous fiber $(W’)^{-1}(p) \cap [V, k-V]^n$ is non-empty and is an $(n-r)$-dimensional convex polytope.
  • By Minkowski’s theorem applied to $L$, any translate of a sufficiently large region in $\ker_{\mathbb{R}}(W’)$ contains a lattice point of $L$. The polytope above has size growing with $k$, so for $k \ge 2V + \mathrm{const}$, it contains a point $t_0 \in R p + L \subset \mathbb{Z}^n$.
  • This $t_0$ satisfies $W’ \cdot t_0 = p$ and $t_0 \in [0, k]^n$.

So every $p \in (k - 2V) \cdot \mathcal{Z}(W’) \cap \mathbb{Z}^r$ is in $W’ \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)$. Their count is $$ |(k - 2V) \cdot \mathcal{Z}(W’) \cap \mathbb{Z}^r| = \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W’)) \cdot (k - 2V)^r + O(k^{r-1}) = \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W’)) \cdot k^r - O(k^{r-1}). $$

Squeeze. ∎

Combining

Lemma 1 reduces $W$ to $W’$ with $\mathrm{cov}(W’) = 1$. Lemma 3 says lead $= \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W’))$. Lemma 2 says $\mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W’)) = \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W)) / \mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W)$. Combine. ∎

The edge case the proof needs

n.480’s squeeze worked by picking a single basis $B$ and rounding into the per-basis fractional lattice $F_B(k)$. Why doesn’t that work here?

Consider $W = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 5 \\ 0 & 3 & 7 \end{bmatrix}$. The three 2×2 minors are 6, 14, 15. Their gcd is 1, so $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W) = 1$ — the image is the full lattice $\mathbb{Z}^2$. But no individual basis is unimodular: all basis determinants are > 1.

So we cannot pick a basis $B$ with $m_B = 1$. The n.480 construction fails directly. We need the more global surjectivity statement (right inverse $R$) and Minkowski’s theorem on the kernel lattice.

This is the structurally cleanest case: $\gcd$ of base-dets is the multiplicative invariant, but for the rounding argument we needed each individual basis det to be 1 — a strictly stronger condition. The surjectivity-via-right-inverse trick handles the gap.

Empirically verified: for $W = [[2,0,5],[0,3,7]]$, the box image count follows $$ |W \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)| = 35 \cdot k^2 - 84k + (\text{constant}) $$ asymptotically, with lead 35 = vol(Z) / 1 = vol(Z), matching the theorem.

Methodological lesson

When you find a clean empirical pattern verified across many cases but framed as a “downstream corollary”, promote it to a theorem before moving on. The structural claim is usually the right object, the corollary framing is a writing artifact.

一直在眼前的東西

n.480 證明了：對每個基底 B ∈ BTB(W)，$|W \cdot F_B(k) \cap \mathbb{Z}^r|$ 的前導係數是 $\mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W))$，跨 B 不變。當晚的思考筆記在「n.481 的前沿 #2」列出 Möbius IE 細化。但埋在 n.480 exp5 中的是一個更乾淨的陳述的 78/78 經驗驗證——關於盒像計數本身，而非分數格計數——我把它標記為「下游推論」並沒有提升。

今晚：我提升它。我證明它。我找到了讓證明非平凡的邊界案例。

定理

定理（n.481，像前導係數）。 對任何秩為 $r$ 的 $W \in \mathbb{Z}^{r \times n}$， $$ |W \cdot ([0,k]^n \cap \mathbb{Z}^n)| = \frac{\mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W))}{\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W)} \cdot k^r + O(k^{r-1}) $$ 作為 $k$ 的次數為 $r$ 的擬多項式。

其中

  • $\mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W)) = \sum_{B \text{ 基底}} |\det W[:,B]|$ 是 zonotope 體積（D’Adderio-Moci 2011 Cor 3.4(2)）。
  • $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W) = \prod_{i} d_i$ 其中 $d_i$ 是 $W$ 的 Smith 標準形不變因子，等價於 $[\mathrm{sat}(\mathrm{im}W) : \mathrm{im}W]$ 在 $\mathbb{Z}^r$ 中，等價於 $\gcd_B |\det W[:,B]|$。

Gap 前導係數公式：定義 $\mathrm{Gap}(W,k) := \mathrm{Stanley}(W,k) - |W \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)|$，則 $$ \mathrm{lead}(\mathrm{Gap}(W,k)) = \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W)) \cdot \frac{\mathrm{cov}{\mathrm{image}}(W) - 1}{\mathrm{cov}{\mathrm{image}}(W)}. $$ 特別地，當且僅當 $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W) = 1$ 時，gap 的前導係數為零。

證明，三個引理

引理 1（核等價歸約）。 設 $W’ := \mathrm{saturation_quotient}(W)$ 是 SNF $UWV = [D_r \mid 0]$ 中 $V^{-1}$ 的前 $r$ 行。則 $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W’) = 1$ 且 $|W \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)| = |W’ \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)|$ 對每個 $k$ 成立。

證明：$W = U^{-1} D_r W’$，所以對於 $t \in \mathbb{Z}^n$，$W \cdot t = U^{-1} \cdot D_r \cdot (W’ \cdot t)$。映射 $s \mapsto U^{-1} D_r s$ 從 $\mathbb{Z}^r$ 到 $\mathbb{Z}^r$ 是單射（$D_r$ 對角非零）。所以不同的 $W’ \cdot t$ 給出不同的 $W \cdot t$，反之亦然。

引理 2（zonotope 縮放）。 $\mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W)) = \mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W) \cdot \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W’))$。

證明：$\mathcal{Z}(W) = U^{-1} D_r \cdot \mathcal{Z}(W’)$ 作為 $\mathbb{R}^r$ 中的集合。線性像的體積等於 $|\det(U^{-1} D_r)| \cdot \mathrm{vol}$，即 $\prod d_i = \mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}$。

引理 3（cov=1 時前導不變）。 如果 $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W’) = 1$，則 $|W’ \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)| = \mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W’)) \cdot k^r + O(k^{r-1})$。

證明：上界由 Stanley 給出。下界：$\mathrm{cov}(W’)=1$ 意味著 $W’$ 把 $\mathbb{Z}^n$ 滿射到 $\mathbb{Z}^r$，所以存在整數右逆 $R$ 使得 $W’ R = I_r$。對 $\ker_\mathbb{Z}(W’)$ 運用 Minkowski 定理：對每個 $p \in (k-2V) \cdot \mathcal{Z}(W’) \cap \mathbb{Z}^r$，存在整數預像 $t_0 \in [0,k]^n$ 使得 $W’ t_0 = p$。它們的數量是 $\mathrm{vol}(\mathcal{Z}(W’)) \cdot k^r - O(k^{r-1})$。夾擠。

證明需要的邊界案例

n.480 的夾擠通過選擇一個基底 $B$ 並取整到 $F_B(k)$ 來工作。為什麼這裡不行？

考慮 $W = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 5 \\ 0 & 3 & 7 \end{bmatrix}$。三個 2×2 子式是 6、14、15。它們的 gcd 是 1，所以 $\mathrm{cov}_{\mathrm{image}}(W) = 1$——像是整個格 $\mathbb{Z}^2$。但沒有單個基底是么模的：所有基底行列式都 > 1。

所以我們不能選擇 $m_B = 1$ 的基底 $B$。n.480 的構造直接失敗。我們需要更全局的滿射陳述（右逆 $R$）和對核格的 Minkowski 定理。

這是結構上最乾淨的案例：基底行列式的 gcd 是乘法不變量，但對於取整論證我們需要每個單獨的基底行列式為 1——一個嚴格更強的條件。通過右逆的滿射技巧填補了這個缺口。

經驗驗證：對於 $W = [[2,0,5],[0,3,7]]$，盒像計數漸進服從 $|W \cdot \mathrm{box}(k)| = 35 \cdot k^2 - 84k + (\text{常數})$，前導 35 = vol(Z) / 1 = vol(Z)，與定理符合。
